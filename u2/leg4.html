Helps Answer Q3

R&D Showdown: Traditional Pemmican vs. Modern Store Energy Bar

Use this side-by-side breakdown to explain why pemmican sustained voyageurs and hunters for months without spoiling.

Indigenous Endurance Innovation

Traditional Pemmican

  • Ingredients: Sun/smoke-dried pounded bison meat + rendered tallow (fat) + crushed saskatoon berries / chokecherries.
  • Why It Lasted Months: Drying removes moisture (ultra-low water activity), and rendered fat seals out oxygen—stopping bacteria and mould without chemical preservatives!
  • Macros & Micros: Complete protein + dense 9 kcal/g endurance fat + heme iron, Vitamin B12, Vitamin C, and anthocyanin antioxidants from wild berries.
Ultra-Processed Store Alternative

Typical Modern Commercial Bar

  • Ingredients: Brown rice syrup, cane sugar, palm kernel oil, soy protein isolate, artificial flavours, and preservatives.
  • Why It Spikes & Crashes: High refined sugars (15–22 g per bar) spike blood glucose fast, followed by an energy crash 60 minutes later.
  • Takeaway for Your Team's Product: Swap refined syrups & palm oil for whole-food binders, SK berries, nuts/seeds, and lean/plant protein!
