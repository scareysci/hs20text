Science Primer 3A: How the Mifflin-St Jeor Equation Works

Helps Answer Q1 & Q2
1. Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

Minimum energy burned in 24 hours at total rest (heart, lungs, brain, body heat):

  • Male: (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) + 5
  • Female: (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) − 161
2. Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor

  • 1.2 = Sedentary (desk/study, little exercise)
  • 1.375 = Lightly Active (light exercise 1–3 days/wk)
  • 1.55 = Moderately Active (moderate sports 3–5 days/wk)
  • 1.725 = Very Active (hard sports/training 6–7 days/wk)
  • 1.9 = Extra Active (heavy physical labour / 14-hr harvest shifts)

Science Primer 3B: Calories (4–4–9) & Health Canada's AMDR

Helps Answer Q3–Q6
Carbohydrates • 4 kcal/g
Health Canada AMDR: 45–65% of daily calories

Main fuel for your brain and muscles. Whole grains, fruit, and oats add fibre; pop and candy are mostly simple sugars.

Protein • 4 kcal/g
Health Canada AMDR: 10–35% of daily calories

Repairs muscles, builds enzymes/hormones, and keeps you full longer (eggs, chicken, beef, Greek yogurt, lentils, tofu).

Fats • 9 kcal/g
Health Canada AMDR: 20–35% of daily calories

More than double the calories per gram! Needed for brain health and vitamins A, D, E, K (nuts, avocado, olive oil, salmon).

Science Primer 3C: Official Health Canada Labelling Rules

Both Partners • Q7
The 5% / 15% Rule:

5% DV or less is a little (aim here for Sat Fat, Sugars, Sodium). 15% DV or more is a lot (great for Fibre, Potassium, Calcium, Iron).

Front-of-Package Warning:

In Canada, foods reaching ≥15% DV for Saturated Fat, Sugars, or Sodium must show a black-and-white "High in..." symbol on the front!

Ingredients & Allergens:

List ingredients in descending order by weight (heaviest first) and declare priority allergens (milk, soy, wheat, peanuts, tree nuts, eggs).
